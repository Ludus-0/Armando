03

Reading: identify and use the tenses

When Maya accepted a job at a small publishing company, she had never worked in an office before. During her first month, she made several mistakes, but her manager encouraged her to learn from them. By the end of the year, Maya had become one of the most reliable members of the team. She has recently started managing a new project, although she has not led a team this large before. Last Tuesday, the company announced that the project would be presented at an international conference. Maya felt nervous at first, but she has prepared carefully and believes the team is ready. She has worked at the company for eighteen months, and she learned most of her skills during her first six months.

1. What kind of workplace did Maya join?

2. How did her manager respond to her early mistakes?

3. What had changed by the end of the year?

4. What new responsibility has Maya taken on?

5. What did the company announce last Tuesday?

6. Why might Maya feel nervous, and what has she done to prepare?

7. Copy one sentence from the text that uses the Present Perfect.

8. Explain why the text uses “Last Tuesday” with a past tense.
